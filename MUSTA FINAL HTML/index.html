About DJ Musta Music

DJ Musta Music is Uganda's premier destination for streaming, discovering and downloading the hottest Ugandan songs. Built around the rhythm of local culture, the platform celebrates kadongo kamu, gospel, afrobeat, dancehall, reggae, and the latest wave of urban Ugandan sound.

More than a music library, DJ Musta is a movement for artists, fans, and the wider creative scene. It gives emerging talent a stage, keeps listeners connected to fresh releases, and turns every stream into a celebration of Uganda's sound and identity.

From trending anthems to underground gems, DJ Musta curates music that reflects the energy of the streets, clubs, churches, and homes across Uganda. The goal is simple: bring people closer to the music they love while helping new voices grow.
